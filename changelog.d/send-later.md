### Added
- Send later: with Settings → Chat → Send later button on, a clock beside Send schedules the
  message for a time you pick, tomorrow at 9:00, or when the agent's plan limit resets. The PC
  keeps it and sends it then with every device closed, waiting for the agent's turn to end; it is
  not sent into a pane that is gone or now runs another session. Scheduled messages show above the
  chat's queued ones on every device, with Send now, Edit and Discard.
