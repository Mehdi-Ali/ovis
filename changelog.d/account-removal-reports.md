### Added
- Removing a device from your account now shows, for every PC, whether that PC reports it removed,
  and the device that removed it brings the removal to each PC it connects to until every PC has
  it, even one whose mailbox never got it.
- "It was lost or stolen" when removing a device lists the devices it added, so you can remove them
  too, one by one.

### Fixed
- An old removal that reached a PC late no longer blocks a device that was paired again on that PC.
